1. Fill a bowl halfway with water and crack two eggs into it.

2. Beat the eggs thoroughly with chopsticks.

3. Add chopped green onions, Dashida (Korean seasoning powder), salt, and other seasonings.

4. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and poke a few small holes in it. Microwave for 10 minutes.
